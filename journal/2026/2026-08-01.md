## Discoveries

### Calibration must be explicit and falsifiable.

The Confidence Calibration Model was defined:

- Log-odds: `L = L0 + Σ wᵢeᵢ`
- Confidence: `S = 1/(1+e⁻ᴸ)`
- Calibrated confidence: `C_final = [α·S + (1−α)·C]·(1−ECE)`

Parameters `α`, `M`, and `L0` were given defaults, with `L0 = 0` unless a documented base rate exists.

### Evidence has quality classes.

Evidence Quality classes Q1–Q4 were defined, each mapping to a weight range `wᵢ`.

Weights are anchored to quality: higher-quality evidence contributes more to confidence.

### Falsification became part of the output contract.

Every Hypothesis must now declare an explicit falsification criterion.

A hypothesis without a condition that could disprove it is not a scientific artifact.

### Business claims were corrected.

"Seven validated Cognitive Principles" became "Seven formalized Cognitive Principles."

The Principles are established by formalization and fallibilism, not by empirical validation.
