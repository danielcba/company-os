## Discoveries

### The principle defines what is possible.

A principle establishes the boundaries of what can happen.

It is the law of the space.

### The context defines the conditions.

Within what is possible, the context determines which conditions are active.

The context is the situation, interpreted.

### The pattern is the solution that emerges under those conditions.

A pattern is not invented.

It emerges when principles and context allow it.

This was the first glimpse of a cognitive grammar: not isolated facts, but a system of conditions and emergences.

---

### The first concepts appeared.

Observation, Evidence, Context.

Reality is captured by Observation.
Observations are organized into Evidence.
Evidence is interpreted into Context.

A flow was born:

```
Reality → Observation → Evidence → Context
```

---

### The Cognitive Lexicon was named.

The shared vocabulary of the project has a name: the Cognitive Lexicon.

It is not a glossary of terms.
It is the beginning of a cognitive system.
