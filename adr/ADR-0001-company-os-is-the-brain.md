# Architecture Decision Record 0001

Title: Company OS is the Brain

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-07

---

## Context

Company OS began as a research project about knowledge, concepts, patterns, and intelligence.

As the Cognitive Lexicon matured, a foundational decision became unavoidable:

> What is Company OS, ultimately?

The alternatives considered:

- A documentation framework for cognitive ideas.
- A knowledge management system.
- A reasoning engine.
- A software library.

None of these captured the full scope of what the project had become.

---

## Decision

**Company OS is the cognitive center of an organization.**

It is the brain: the subsystem that perceives, reasons, decides, and learns on behalf of the organization it serves.

Company OS is not a feature, a library, or a documentation set.

It is the architecture of organizational cognition.

This means:

1. Every perception of the organization passes through Company OS.
2. Every interpretation is governed by the Cognitive Principles.
3. Every decision is the result of the Cognitive Flow.
4. Every outcome is consolidated into Memory (planned) and used to learn.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Gives the project a stable architectural identity.
- Every future component has a defined place: perception, reasoning, action, or learning.
- Positions Company OS as a platform, not a product.
- Aligns engineering with a discipline: the engineering of cognition.

### Negative

- Imposes a high standard of rigor on every component.
- Requires the separation of perception from action authority.
- Delays implementation until the architecture is coherent.
- Demands that software serve the architecture, never the opposite.

---

## Compliance

This ADR is the foundational premise of Company OS.

No component may be added that contradicts the role of Company OS as the cognitive center.

The architecture should guide the code, never the opposite.
