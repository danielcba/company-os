# Brand

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Owner: Company OS

---

## Purpose

Define the identity, voice, and visual language of Company OS.

The brand must reflect what the architecture is: rigorous, principled, and human.

---

## Brand Essence

Company OS is the discipline of engineering cognition.

It builds the brain of an organization.

The brand expresses rigor before flash, understanding before action.

---

## Name

**Company OS**

Short, precise, structural.

It communicates a system — not a feature, not a product, not a document.

The name positions the project as an operating layer: what the organization runs on.

### Naming Distinction

The name "Company OS" also exists in other software projects (an npm package and an AI-assistant skill). Company OS is none of those.

Company OS is a cognitive architecture framework: a lexicon, an ontology, and an engineering discipline for organizational cognition.

It is not an operating system, a graphic engine, or an agent tool. The distinction is the architecture: it defines how an organization perceives, reasons, decides, and learns — for humans and AI agents alike.

---

## Positioning Statement

> Company OS is the cognitive center of an organization.
> It defines not only what an intelligent system knows, but how it thinks.

## Voice

- **Precise.** Every term has one meaning.
- **Calm.** No hype, no drama.
- **Structural.** Focus on how systems are built.
- **Human.** The architecture exists to serve decisions made by people.

Language conventions:

- Define before using a term.
- State confidence when making a claim.
- Prefer "the architecture guides the code" over "best practices".

---

## Visual Identity

Planned assets:

- A flow mark representing the Cognitive Flow: `Reality → Observation → ... → Decision → Memory`
- A monochrome palette: deep neutral tones with one accent for Confidence or Decision.
- A diagram system that mirrors the ontology and stays synchronized with it.

---

## Anti-Brand

Company OS is not:

- A dashboard product.
- A consulting brand with jargon.
- A black-box AI promise.

Anything that obscures understanding contradicts the brand.
