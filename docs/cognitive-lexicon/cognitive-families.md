# Cognitive Families

Version: 1.0
Status: Official
Owner: Company OS

---

## Purpose

A Cognitive Family is a grouping of Core Concepts that share a common cognitive orientation.

Families give the Lexicon an internal grammar.

A concept does not merely exist.
Every concept belongs to a family, and every family expresses one orientation of cognition.

---

## The Four Cognitive Families

### Perception

Perception is the orientation that brings reality into the system.

| Concept | Capability |
|---|---|
| Observation | Capture |
| Evidence | Organize |
| Context | Explain |

Perception does not judge.
It establishes what exists, how it is organized, and what it means under the most coherent model.

---

### Reasoning

Reasoning is the orientation that transforms understanding into structure and explanation.

| Concept | Capability |
|---|---|
| Pattern | Generalize |
| Anomaly | Detect Deviation |
| Hypothesis | Predict |
| Insight | Restructure |

Reasoning does not act on the world.
It acts on knowledge.

---

### Action

Action is the orientation that converts understanding into committed behavior.

| Concept | Capability |
|---|---|
| Recommendation | Propose |
| Decision | Commit |

Action is the point where cognition meets the world.

---

### Learning

Learning is the orientation that improves the system through time and outcome.

| Concept | Capability |
|---|---|
| Confidence | Calibrate |
| Memory | Consolidate |

Learning closes the cognitive loop.
Every cycle produces the material for the next cycle to be better.

Learning includes the following sub-capabilities:

| Sub-capability | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Evaluation | Manages Hypothesis lifecycle (candidate → confirmed / falsified / insufficient) |
| Consolidation | Computes calibration feedback from Decision outcomes |
| Pattern Refinement | Attributes outcomes to Patterns, recommends keep/degrade/deactivate |
| Context Revision | Attributes outcomes to Contexts, recommends keep/review/consider_competitor |
| Insight Transformation | Journals Insight changes, classifies revised/stable/unchanged |

---

## Metacognition

Metacognition is not a fourth family.
It is a cross-cutting orientation: cognition applied to cognition.

Confidence is the first metacognitive capability of the Lexicon.

Metacognition monitors the quality of reasoning, detects failures, and redirects the system.

---

## Rules for Families

- Every Core Concept belongs to exactly one family.
- A family is defined by its orientation, not by its members.
- The Lexicon may gain families as the ontology evolves, but the four founding families are stable.
- Families describe cognition, not software modules.

---

## Evolution Notes

Future versions may define:

- New families revealed by discovery
- Cross-family mechanisms (e.g., metacognition applied to perception)
