# Insight

Version: 1.0
Status: Official
Owner: Company OS
Cognitive Family: Reasoning
Capability: Restructure

---

## Definition

An Insight is a novel understanding that results from restructuring the relationship between existing concepts or knowledge.

An insight is not new information.
It is a new organization of information that was already available.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of Insight is to produce understanding that cannot be reached by incremental reasoning alone.

Insight reconfigures the cognitive space and opens possibilities that were previously invisible.

---

## Why It Matters

Reasoning within a fixed frame can solve defined problems.

Insight changes the frame.

It is the cognitive mechanism by which Company OS discovers that a different mental model explains the evidence better, or that two previously unrelated patterns belong to the same structure.

Concepts are stable.
Intelligence lives in the transformations between them.

Insight is the visible result of a transformation between concepts.

---

## Cognitive Contract

### Input

- Active Context
- Active Hypotheses
- Existing knowledge structures

### Transformation

Restructure the relationship between existing knowledge elements to produce a more coherent or more general understanding.

### Output

- A novel understanding (Insight)
- An update to the active mental model

---

## Relationships

### Produced By

Hypothesis evaluation and pattern integration

### Refines

Context and Hypothesis

### Leads To

Recommendation

---

## Examples

### Example 1

Situation: Backup failures, disk pressure, and slow response times are each treated as separate problems.

Insight: All three symptoms are consequences of a single constraint: the storage controller is saturated.

The evidence did not change.
The organization of the evidence changed.

### Example 2

Situation: Two anomaly streams are monitored independently.

Insight: Both streams are generated by the same compromised account.

---

## Non-Examples

"A new fact was learned."

That is a new Observation, not an Insight.

Insight restructures existing knowledge rather than adding facts.

"A hypothesis was confirmed."

That is validation, not necessarily Insight.

Insight changes the structure of understanding.

---

## Design Implications

Company OS must allow multiple interpretations to coexist so that restructuring remains possible.

Insight cannot be forced or scheduled.

The architecture should:

- Detect when the current frame fails repeatedly,
- Trigger restructuring of the mental model,
- Record the transformation that produced the insight.

Journaling transformations, not only facts, is an architectural requirement.
